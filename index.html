Who is Shivansh?

A Bit About Me

I'm an AI engineer working as a Jr. Data Scientist at Harij Softech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. I build prototypes for document extraction, local language-model applications and computer vision.

My work spans LightOnOCR and Ollama invoice extraction, FAISS-based policy retrieval, YOLO and PaddleOCR pipelines, and local voice assistants. I connect model outputs to usable workflows with Python, FastAPI and PostgreSQL.

I enjoy the engineering details: separating OCR errors from parsing errors, handling limited GPU memory, designing label taxonomies and making results inspectable. The projects below link to code and explain what their evaluation covers.

I hold an MCA in AI & ML from Chandigarh University. Earlier analytics work covers public supply-chain and retail datasets using Python, SQL and Power BI.

01

Invoice Extractor — Local Document AI

LightOnOCR-2 + llama3.2 · runs on a 4 GB GPU

A PDF-to-JSON prototype that replaces layout-specific repair rules with OCR markdown and local language-model parsing. Stage batching limits model swapping on a small GPU. The documented tuning result is 93.9% strict cell accuracy (398/424 cells, 16 pages). A separate check matched 98.6% of extracted values to embedded PDF text on 24 unseen sample invoices; it does not measure missing values or extraction completeness.

02

Voice AI Agent — Local LLM with Real-Time Speech

Local speech pipeline · appointment-booking prototype

A local voice-assistant prototype using faster-whisper for speech recognition, Silero VAD for turn detection, Ollama for responses and Piper for speech synthesis over LiveKit. Sentence streaming and audio prefetching reduce waiting between chunks. Sub-second barge-in was observed during development; it measures interruption handling, not complete response latency. Repository logs contain text-only response timings across four local models.

03

ALPR — Real-Time License Plate Reader

YOLOv8 + SORT + PaddleOCR · documented detector training

A video-processing prototype combining vehicle detection, SORT tracking, plate detection and confidence-weighted OCR character voting. The custom YOLOv8s training run used 3,082 training images and 418 validation images; its final log records 97.5% precision and 98.7% mAP@50. These scores describe plate detection, not complete plate-text accuracy. The repository also includes a separate pretrained plate detector and a companion frame-detection project.

07

RC Extractor — Indian Vehicle Documents

Ordered token extraction · confidence-based review flags

A rule-based prototype for Indian vehicle registration certificates. PaddleOCR feeds an ordered pool of field detectors: stricter patterns reserve tokens before broader patterns inspect what remains. A Streamlit interface and batch CSV mode support inspection. AUTO_APPROVED and MANUAL_REVIEW are heuristic workflow labels; confidence thresholds are not calibrated probabilities of correctness.

Head-Pose Video Monitor

MediaPipe FaceMesh + solvePnP · head-pose heuristics

An offline, single-face video prototype that estimates head pose using MediaPipe landmarks and OpenCV solvePnP. Configurable yaw, pitch and duration thresholds produce temporally smoothed posture labels, annotated video and CSV summaries. These labels describe head-position heuristics; they are not validated measurements of attention, engagement or productivity.
